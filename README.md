# cardbillcheck

A credit card bill checker that flags expenses that appear in your bill but don't appear in your record.

I track my spending in a an app that allows export, but reconciling it against the actual
credit card bill was the chore I kept putting off. So I built this to do it
in a few seconds instead.

## 🔗 Try it

**[mctasuncion88.github.io/cardbillcheck](https://mctasuncion88.github.io/cardbillcheck/)**

No install, no sign-up. Everything runs in your browser — your files never
get uploaded anywhere.

## How it works

1. Upload one or more **Excel/CSV files** from your expense tracker covering the relevant time period of your bill
2. Upload your **credit card bill as a PDF**
3. Hit **Reconcile**

It compares the two and highlights anything on the bill that isn't in your
tracker — so you can review unfamiliar or forgotten charges before you pay.

## Why I made it

Mostly to make this chore faster and less time-consuming.

## Notes

- Built as a single self-contained HTML page
- Your financial data stays on your device — nothing is sent to a server
